Before You Apply

Know how our contributor model works.

Project-Based Opportunities

  • Current contributor opportunities are project-based.
  • They are not traditional fixed-salary employment positions.
  • Profit-sharing depends on the specific project and contribution.
  • Terms will be discussed and agreed before project work begins.

Professional Responsibility

  • Being accepted as a contributor does not guarantee Team DEEPBYTO membership.
  • Team membership is based on performance and long-term potential.
  • Confidential project and client information must be protected.
  • Publicly sharing confidential project information requires appropriate permission.
