The Dream About Jensen Huang and NVIDIA: A Strange Encounter on November 10

A Personal Dream Story by Abdul Sami • DEEPBYTO • November 10, 2026

Introduction

On November 10, 2026, I experienced a dream that I found particularly unusual and memorable. It involved Jensen Huang, the CEO and founder of NVIDIA, one of the world's most recognized technology companies.

What made this dream interesting was not simply seeing a globally recognized technology leader. It was the situation, the conversation, his emotions, and the way he approached me.

I had no idea why my mind had created such a specific scenario, but the details remained in my memory after waking up.

A Conversation About the World's Biggest Companies

The dream began in an ordinary place. I was standing with a friend, casually talking to him. It was not a corporate office, a conference hall, or any special business environment.

I was standing comfortably, with my hands in my pockets, smiling and talking to my friend.

During our conversation, I was explaining something unusual that seemed to be happening around the world.

In the dream, owners and CEOs of major companies were being asked whether they wanted to continue leading their companies or leave their positions. It felt like a major decision-making process involving business leaders from around the world.

I was discussing this situation with my friend when something unexpected happened.

The Unexpected Arrival of Jensen Huang

Suddenly, someone approached me.

It was Jensen Huang.

I immediately recognized him. He came towards me with a very happy and energetic expression. His behaviour was unusual: he seemed extremely excited, almost jumping with happiness, and appeared close to tears.

He started talking to me as if we already knew each other. There was no formal introduction, no hesitation, and no feeling of distance between us.

He told me that it was now his turn.

In the context of the dream, he meant that he was now being asked to make a decision about his position and his company.

He seemed genuinely happy about this moment.

"Why Would I Leave NVIDIA?"

I asked him what he was going to do.

He responded with confidence and excitement, explaining that he was leading a very large company and questioning why he would leave it.

His response was essentially:

"Why would I leave? I have such a big company."

I agreed with him and told him that he should not leave.

I explained that he was the owner and leader of a major company, and that he should continue leading it.

He maintained the same positive attitude. The conversation felt natural, almost like two friends discussing an important decision.

What I remember most clearly is his happiness and his repeated confidence that he had no reason to leave NVIDIA.

An Ordinary Meeting With an Extraordinary Person

One of the most interesting details was my own behaviour during the encounter.

I was not nervous or overwhelmed. I was standing comfortably, smiling, with my hands in my pockets, just as I had been while talking to my friend.

Even though I was speaking with one of the most recognizable figures in the technology industry, I treated the interaction like a normal conversation.

Jensen Huang was the only major business leader I remember personally meeting in that dream.

I did not meet the owners of other companies. The entire encounter was specifically connected to Jensen Huang and NVIDIA.

Sharing the Dream With My Family

After the main encounter, I also remember discussing the dream with my grandfather.

I was explaining what had happened and telling him about Jensen Huang's visit, his excitement, and our conversation about NVIDIA.

There was also a brief conversation involving my brother.

Another detail that remained in my memory was a numerical comparison involving NVIDIA and Apple. I remember mentioning numbers that sounded approximately like 4.98 for NVIDIA and 5.28 for Apple.

I cannot confidently explain what those numbers represented in the dream. They may simply have been part of the unusual conversation or dream imagery.

Why Did NVIDIA Appear in My Dream?

This is the question I still find interesting.

Why NVIDIA?

Why Jensen Huang?

Why was he the only business leader who personally approached me?

And why was the entire conversation about whether he should continue leading his company?

I have an interest in technology, artificial intelligence, entrepreneurship, and building businesses. As someone working towards building DEEPBYTO, I often think about companies, leadership, innovation, and the future of technology.

Perhaps these interests influenced the dream. However, I cannot say with certainty why my mind created this particular story.

Dreams can combine memories, emotions, personal interests, and imagination in unexpected ways.

A Personal Reflection

The dream took place during a period when I was also experiencing significant personal stress.

I was thinking about financial limitations, respect, opportunities, and the desire to build something meaningful. There were moments when I felt that having financial success could change how people treated me.

Perhaps this emotional background also influenced the dream.

Still, the encounter with Jensen Huang was different from an ordinary dream. The conversation felt natural, his emotions were vivid, and the details stayed with me.

I do not consider this dream proof of a future event or a prediction. I simply find the experience interesting enough to document and share.

Final Thoughts

Sometimes, the most memorable dreams are not the ones involving extraordinary places or impossible situations. They are the ones that feel surprisingly real.

For me, November 10, 2026, became memorable because of a dream involving Jensen Huang and NVIDIA.

I remember his excitement, his words, his confidence, and the simple question that defined our conversation:

"Why would I leave NVIDIA?"

It was a dream, but it left me with a story worth writing down.

This article documents a personal dream and subjective experience. It does not claim to predict real-world events or establish any connection between the dream and actual corporate decisions.
